About SkyNest

A community built around flexible living and modern city life.

Our story

SkyNest Co-Living Spaces is a modern co-living company based in Singapore. We provide flexible short-term and long-term accommodation for young professionals, digital nomads and international students.

Demand for our rooms has grown quickly as more people move abroad to work and study. We believe that where you live should support your goals, your wellbeing and your friendships.

Who we are for

SkyNest is a home for many different journeys.

International students

An affordable, safe place to live while you study, with quiet study pods nearby.
